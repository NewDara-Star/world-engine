How it was made

The car was turned into a grid of distances (2 cm cells). A program started from one plain box (run A) or a six-box hatchback layout written before the run (run B), adjusted every number until the error stopped falling, then kept adding a box wherever the car was least covered. Every box is mirrored left to right and may tilt forward or back. Paint is one colour per box, read from the original texture.

What it can't do yet

  • No texture: lights, windows and trim are only as good as one colour per box.
  • Thin parts (mirrors) and inward curves (wheel arches) are hard for boxes that only add together.
  • The speed number here is your device, not the reference phone.

What it's good for

  • Tiny files: the shape list is hundreds of bytes, not hundreds of kilobytes.
  • Variety: stretch one number and you have a longer car, without a new model.
  • A clean starting shape for Meshy models before they're cut down.
