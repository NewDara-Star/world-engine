The shape factory

These are 24 of the made-up cars the learner studies. The factory invents a new one for every lesson, so it never sees the same car twice, and each comes with its exact answer: which boxes built it. The grey car at the front is your real hatchback, which the learner never studies. It's only used to check how well the lessons carry over.

Why made-up cars

Like Corridor Crew's green-screen keyer trained on generated footage, every example here has a perfect answer, so the learner can be marked exactly. Real cars don't come with their box recipe.

How they're made

  • A body, a cabin, then maybe a bonnet, windscreen, tailgate and bumpers, all sized from real car proportions (4 m long, about 1.75 m wide, 1.45 m tall), with random variation.
  • Up to two random extra boxes, so it doesn't only learn neat cars.

The weak spot

Real cars are smooth; these are blocky. That gap is why the learner's answer is then polished by the search. Making the factory's cars more like real ones is the main way to make it better.
