What each level adds

  • Low: flat light, no shadows, 8 cars, sharp only at 1× pixels, fog at 200 m.
  • Medium: real sky lighting, sun shadows near you, film-like colour, trees, garden walls, 24 cars, up to 1.5× pixels.
  • High: softer, sharper shadows, shading in corners (ambient occlusion), a little glow, more trees, 48 cars, up to 2× pixels, 600 m view.

What's real and what's stand-in

  • Roads and the 4 mapped buildings are real OpenStreetMap data, baked by World data.
  • OpenStreetMap has almost no buildings around your street, so plain houses are placed along every road as stand-ins. They're there so the phone carries a realistic load, not to show what Omole looks like.
  • Cars are the World Drive cars, cut from about 800 KB each to about 140 KB (smaller pictures, packed shapes).

How to read it

60 fps is smooth; 30 fps is playable for driving if it never dips. "Worst 1%" is the stutter you feel. The highest level that stays green is the realism we can afford on this phone. Copy the results and paste them back to me.
