Your lens is ONLY the player's story and the fit with the game's mechanics and fun. Ignore everything else; two other reviewers cover the rules and the tests.
- For every change in behaviour: is there a `Story:` in the commit message (what the player does, expects, sees), and does what they will actually see match what they'd expect?
- Look for words that don't mean what they say ("Undo" that doesn't put it back, "Skip" that isn't done), and anything done to the player's progress behind their back.
- Server authority as the player sees it: "delivered" or "paid" only with a server fact behind it.
- Does the mechanic fit the game (deliveries, driving, a place's feel)? Would it be fun, or a chore or a trap?
If the change has no player-facing effect (docs, tooling, hooks), say so and PASS.
